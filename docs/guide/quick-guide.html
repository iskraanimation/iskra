A free and open-source 2D animation app, with the storyboard built in.
Board the film, animate each shot frame by frame or as a cut-out rig, and render the shots out one by one.

One file, one folder

A project is one file, Film.iskra. When it is first saved it goes in a folder of its name, with folders for what comes with it:

Film/
  Film.iskra
  Images/  Audio/  Palettes/
  Library/  Shots/  Renders/

Everything the film holds (storyboards, shots, drawings, palettes, sound) is inside the one .iskra file.

Saving

Cmd+S saves, Cmd+Shift+S saves as. Autosave is on: a minute after you change something it is saved. Switch it off or change the wait in Settings ▸ General.

Getting Iskra

Iskra is built from its source code with Rust 1.95 or newer:

cargo run --release

On Linux, install the windowing headers first (listed in the README).

3The four pages

One project holds the storyboard, its shots and their media. The bar at the foot of the window switches between four pages, each laid out for one part of the work.

Media Storyboard Animation Export

Media

What the project holds: its bins of storyboards, shots, pictures and sound, each shot's status, and the files on disk. Shots are sent out to animators and brought back here (see 4).

Storyboard

The film as a timeline of shots, with the sound under them. Draw the boards, time the shots, play the animatic (see 5).

Animation

One shot: its layers, drawings, keys, pegs, rigs and effects, on its own timeline and node view (see 10–15).

Export

Render settings, a viewer, and the render queue, which knows which shots have changed since they were last rendered (see 16).

How the work flows

  • A storyboard is a timeline of shots, as a sequence is in a film editor. Playing it is the animatic.
  • A shot is an animation of its own: its layers, drawings, timeline and node graph. A shot is in one storyboard at most; to use it twice, duplicate it.
  • Opening follows the item. Double-click a storyboard to open it on the Storyboard page, a shot to open it on the Animation page. There is no export or import between boarding and animating.
  • Boards are kept. Layers drawn on the Storyboard page are board layers. On the Animation page they are at the top of the timeline, faded and locked, as reference; the storyboard keeps playing them, and renders use the animation layers.
  • Each page remembers what it has open: going back from a shot to the Storyboard page finds the storyboard with the playhead on that shot.
Panels on any page: the Workspace menu shows or hides every panel and page. Tab hides every panel but the viewport, and brings them back.

Bringing things in

File ▸ Import Media… takes pictures (PNG, JPEG, TIFF, BMP, WebP, TGA, PSD) and sound (WAV). File ▸ Import Character… takes a layered Photoshop or OpenRaster file as a rig (see 14).

The life of a shot

Each shot has a status: Board → Out → Animation → Post → Final, set in Properties with the shot picked. Sending out and bringing back set it for you.

Sending shots out

Storyboard ▸ Send Shot Out… saves each picked shot as a project file of its own, with its drawings, palettes and its stretch of the sound, for an animator to open like any project. The shot is marked Out.

Bring Back… takes the file when it returns: it shows what the returned shot changes, then takes the shot's place, keeping its name and its clip. The project's settings stay; the palette gets the swatches it lacks. One undo brings the old version back.

Boarding, step by step

  • Cmd+Shift+N adds a shot after this one, as long, with the playhead in it.
  • Drag its right end to set its length.
  • Add a layer, draw; add drawings on later frames.
  • Lay the sound on the tracks below.
  • Play: the film plays from shot to shot.

Transitions

Right-click a shot ▸ Transition In or Transition Out: Cross Dissolve, Dip to Black, Dip to White. Drag its end to make it longer.

In and out points, handles

A shot has a length, and its clip shows the part of it that is in the cut. Dragging a clip's end inwards trims it; outwards it uncovers the frames beyond, its handles. Nothing is deleted by trimming. On the Animation page the animator has all the frames, with the cut marked.

Panels and notes

Storyboard ▸ New Panel at the Playhead splits a shot into panels; the Shot Notes panel holds each panel's action, dialogue and slug. File ▸ Export Storyboard PDF… prints the boards.

6The tools

The tools strip runs down the left of the Storyboard and Animation pages. Each tool's settings are in Tool Properties and in the bar over the viewport.

 Select Alt+SClick a drawing to select all of it, or drag a box round a part (or use the Lasso). Drag to move, a corner to scale, the handle above to turn.
 Cutter Alt+TDraw an outline across lines to cut them along it; what is inside is selected, to move or delete. With Alt it is taken away. A flick across a line takes it off to the next crossing.
 Brush Alt+BDraws with the brush in hand (see 7), with a pen's pressure on macOS, Windows and Linux.
 Eraser Alt+ERubs out. A pen's eraser end is the eraser too.
 Paint Bucket Alt+IClick inside lines to fill the colour art there with the swatch in hand, out to the lines. Small gaps in the lines are closed.
 ShapeLine Alt+\, Rectangle Alt+7, Ellipse Alt+=, Polyline, Triangle, Star, Arrow, Spiral, Grid: dragged out and drawn with the brush in hand.
 EyedropperClick the drawing to take the swatch it is painted with, then go back to the tool you had.
 Gradient/TextureClick an area painted with a gradient or a picture to see where it lies; drag its ends to move it (see 9).
 Point Editor Alt+QOn vector art: click a shape to see its points, drag them and their handles; click its line to add a point.
 Text Alt+YOn a text layer: click to type, or drag a box whose lines wrap. The Text panel sets the font, size and colour.
 Zoom Alt+ZDrag right or down to zoom in, left or up out; click for a step in, Alt-click out; Shift-drag a box to fill the view with it.
 CameraDrag to pan the shot's camera, drag its round handle to roll it; its zoom and keys are in Tool Properties (see 15).
 Bones Alt+JLay a chain of bones on the layer's drawing and edit it at rest; the Transform tool poses it (see 14).
 Transform Shift+TDrag to move the peg with what it carries, drag its round handle to turn it (see 11).
Quick switches: L and K switch between drawing on the line art and on the colour art. 2 and 1 zoom in and out, V and C turn the view, Shift+M puts it back.

General Hard and soft rounds, with and without pressure, airbrushes, a flat calligraphy brush.

Wet Media Watercolour, ink pen, oil bristle, gouache, ink wash.

Stamped tips

In Stamped Tip, Tip from a Picture… takes any PNG as the brush's tip. It is stamped along the line at the Spacing you set, turned with the line or not, with Angle Jitter and Angle Drift; Build Up lets the stamps darken where they overlap. The tip is kept with the brush and in the project.

Bringing brushes in

Drawing ▸ Import Brushes… reads Photoshop brushes (.abr) and Iskra's own brush files. Export Brushes… writes them for another machine.

Brush slots: put a brush in one of five slots (its menu ▸ Slot) and pick it with Drawing ▸ Brush in Slot 1–5; give those a key in Settings.

8Art layers, bitmap and vector

A drawing has art layers inside it, one over another. Colour is painted under the lines, so it never covers them, and the lines stay clean.

Line art LThe lines. Where you draw unless you choose.
Colour art KThe colour under the lines: the Paint Bucket fills it, out to the lines above.
OverartOver the lines: highlights, shines, notes.

Pick the art layer with the four buttons on the viewport's bar; the last shows all of them alike. View ▸ Show the Line Art Alone (and the others) hides the rest while you work.

Bitmap or vector, layer by layer

Bitmap layers are pixels, at a resolution higher than the shot's if you like (Settings ▸ Format ▸ New layer resolution), so a camera that pushes in stays sharp. Each pixel remembers the swatch it was painted with, so changing the swatch recolours it (see 9).

The Mixer Brush paints free colour, pixels of their own colours that mix as paint does, for painted backgrounds.

Vector layers keep the shapes you draw. They stay sharp at any size, the Point Editor moves their points, and the Select tool's Pieces pick the parts of a line between crossings.

Add one with Drawing ▸ New Vector Layer or the timeline's Add Layer. Make Vector Layer… traces a bitmap layer's drawings, with a preview; Make Bitmap Layer turns vector art into pixels.

Text layers (Drawing ▸ New Text Layer) hold boxes of text, typed with the Text tool and laid out with a real font. Cmd+Shift+T turns a text into vector shapes, to draw on.

Guides for drawing

Drawing ▸ New Ruler and New 1-, 2- or 3-Point Perspective put a guide on the shot: with it in hand, a stroke runs along the line the guide gives through where it began. Drag its handles to set it, and pick it again from Tool Properties' Guide list. Put the Drawing Guide Away lets the brush go free.

11Keyframes, pegs and motion

Pegs carry layers, and keyframes on a peg move it through the shot. The path it takes is drawn in the viewport, where it can be bent.

Animate a peg

  • Pick the peg (its row, or the layer under it) and the Transform tool Shift+T.
  • Switch Animate on: it goes red, and what you move is keyed on the frame you move it on.
  • Go to another frame and move it again. The frames between are worked out.

With Animate off, a move is the same on every frame: what has keys moves on all of them alike.

Adjust makes a change on one key the same on every key picked on the timeline: move one 20 to the right and they all move 20.

1 2 3
1
Keyframe A red dot on the path. Drag it to move the peg on that frame.
2
Handles Bend the path through a key; Path: Corner at Keyframe breaks it into a corner.
3
Frames One dot a frame: close together it slows, far apart it is quick. Slide them along the path to space the in-betweens.

Easing

Right-click a key on the timeline or use Animation ▸ Keyframe: Linear, Slow In, Slow Out, Slow In and Out, Hold. Hold Keyframes on Twos (or ones, threes) steps the motion as drawn animation does. Cmd+L switches keys between held and animated.

14Rigs, characters and the library

A cut-out character is a tree of pegs and layers, its parts bent by deformers. Rig it once, keep it in the library, and bring it into every shot.

Bring a character in

File ▸ Import Character… reads a layered Photoshop (.psd, .psb) or OpenRaster (.ora, which Krita saves) file: every part becomes a layer, placed as it is in the document, each on a peg of its own.

Tags at the start of a layer's or group's name say more: [merge] flattens a group into one drawing, [slot] makes a group one layer whose drawings are its parts (mouths, hands), [ignore] leaves it out, and [skin Name] makes its parts that skin's drawings. Import Skins… brings a changed design in over the rig, its pegs, bones and animation kept.

Bones

With the Bones tool Alt+J, drag along a part to lay a chain of bones on its drawing. Each bone holds the part of the drawing near it; the parts between two bones are shared between them. Pose it with the Transform tool: a bone turns about its start, and its first joint moves the whole chain. Where the chain has IK on, drag its goal and the bones reach for it.

1 2 3
1
A chain of bones laid along the arm; its joints turn.
2
IK goal A ring with a cross; drag it and the chain reaches.
3
A curve deformer A smooth line the drawing follows as it bends: a tail, hair.

Three kinds of deformer

Bones for limbs; a Curve for what bends along its length; an Envelope, a closed outline round a shape, to squash, stretch and bulge it, as a cheek. Pick the kind in the Bones tool's properties.

The library

The Library panel shows folders of templates (Animation ▸ Add Library Folder…). Drag nodes from the node view or the timeline onto it to save a template; drag a template into a shot to put a copy there. A template's Apply onto the Shot's Own… applies it to the nodes of the same names, as a pose onto a rig.

15The camera and depth

Iskra is 2D, but its layers lie at depths in front of a camera, so a camera move gives parallax, and what is nearer is in front.

Top ViewSide View
1 2 3
1
The camera with what it sees opening out in front of it. Drag it to move its peg.
2
Layers Each drawing is a flat card, a line here, at the depth its pegs give it. Drag a line to move it nearer or farther.
3
Pivot handle of a selected peg: moves it in depth and across, where many lines lie on one another.

The Camera tool

Drag in the viewport to pan the camera, drag its round handle to roll it. Its zoom, and the keys of its moves, are in Tool Properties. Animation ▸ Add Camera Peg gives a shot a camera to move if it has none.

View ▸ Flat View, Not Through the Camera shows the drawings flat, without the camera, to draw on them as they are.

Drawing larger than the shot

Bitmap layers can have more pixels than the shot, so a push-in stays sharp. Vector art is drawn at the size it is seen. When a layer is shown larger than its pixels allow, it is outlined in the viewport as a warning.

Guides over the frame

View ▸ Guide: Action Safe, Title Safe, Rule of Thirds, Centre Cross, 12 Fields; My Guides… for your own. Aspect Mask on the view bar darkens what is outside another frame's shape, as a cinema crop.

  • Each shot's part in the cut, with handles, or each shot whole.
  • One movie of the whole storyboard, or a file per shot.

For the edit

The cut goes to the editor as an edit list: EDL, OpenTimelineIO or FCPXML, with each rendered shot where it lies, the transitions and the sound. The handles let the editor slide a cut.

Without the app

iskra-render Film.iskra --format mov

renders from the command line, for render farms and scripts.

17Make it yours

Every page's layout, every bar and every key can be changed, and kept.

Panels

  • Move: drag a panel by its tab, beside another, into its tabs, or to an edge to split it.
  • Own window: right-click its tab ▸ Detach into Its Own Window, for a second screen. Close that window to put the panel back.
  • Show and hide: Workspace ▸ any panel, or the cross on its tab. Tab hides all but the viewport.

Bars

Each panel's bar is made of parts between the ⋮ grips. Drag a grip to move its part, or into the panel's lower half to make a second bar at its foot. The … at the bar's end shows or hides parts and puts the bar back as it was.

Workspaces

Workspace ▸ Save Workspace As… keeps the page's layout under a name; switch between them in the Workspace menu. Reset Layout brings back the page's own. The app reopens as it was closed.

Settings (Cmd+,)

GeneralYour name, autosave, how long new drawings hold, Animate at start, tablet input.
InterfaceDark or light, the size of the interface, the canvas surround.
Memory and GPUThe playback cache, memory for effects, the graphics card.
ShortcutsThe keymap: Harmony, Animate or TVPaint layouts, and your own keys, for every page or one.
New ProjectsThe resolution and frame rate presets.
FormatThis project's frame rate, pixel aspect, new layer resolution and colour working space (sRGB or linear).

Command palette

Cmd+Shift+P finds any command, brush or script by typing a few letters of its name.

Scripts

Scripts are JavaScript. They edit the project through the app's own commands, so anything the menus do a script can do. Put them in the scripts folder (Scripts ▸ Show Scripts Folder): each becomes a command, in the menus, the command palette and the Shortcuts table.

The Script Console runs a line at a time, to try things. Run Script File… runs one from anywhere. Scripts may only write files if Settings ▸ Scripting lets them.

19Good to know

QUESTIONANSWER
Part of the drawing is bright magenta.It was painted with a swatch that has been removed. Paint it with another, or bring the palette back.
The bucket fills the whole drawing.It fills the colour art out to the line art. Check you are on the colour art (K), and that the lines close; small gaps are closed for you.
I can't draw on a layer.It may be locked (the lock on its row), or a board layer, which is locked on the Animation page. Or the playhead is on an empty cell: draw, and a drawing is made there.
What I moved moved on every frame.Animate was off. Switch it on to key a move on the frame it is made on.
The pen draws as a mouse.The status bar says Pen or Mouse. Choose how tablet input is read in Settings ▸ General; the Log panel says what the pen reports.
A panel is gone.Workspace ▸ the panel's name, or Workspace ▸ Reset Layout. Tab may have hidden the panels.
A shortcut does nothing.The command may not apply now (it is greyed in its menu), or typing in a box takes the keys. Settings ▸ Shortcuts says where a key is given twice.
Playback is slow the first time round.Frames are made as they are first played and kept on the graphics card; the timeline marks those that are ready. Give the playback cache more memory in Settings ▸ Memory and GPU.
Is my work saved?Autosave saves a minute after each change. File ▸ Save As… makes a copy under another name.
Something went wrong.The Log panel (Workspace ▸ Log) says what the app did and what failed. Copy takes all of it, for a report.
Where are my settings?macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Iskra
Windows: %APPDATA%\Iskra
Linux: ~/.config/iskra
Your keymaps, workspaces and brushes are there; copy the folder to keep them.
Can I get my drawings out of a project without Iskra?iskra-extract Film.iskra writes the drawings, the timing and the palette out of a project file.
